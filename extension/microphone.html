MICROPHONE PERMISSION

Enable your microphone

Chrome sometimes needs a normal tab to show this permission. Allow access here and we’ll return you to your source. Then click the microphone beside your take to start dictating.

This setup briefly activates the microphone to grant access, then stops it immediately. It does not record, save, or upload audio.

If you choose “Allow this time”, keep this tab open while using dictation. You can close it when you’re finished. Your draft stays in the sidebar.

This uses Chrome’s default microphone. To use your Mac instead of your iPhone, choose its built-in microphone in Chrome Settings → Privacy and security → Site settings → Microphone before allowing access.
